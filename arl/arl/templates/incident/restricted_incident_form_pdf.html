Human and Organizational Performance Principles
  1. People make mistakes – error is normal. Focus on identifying what process failed rather than who failed.
  2. Blame fixes nothing – focus should not be on blame. Ensure a safe space to allow for honest communication on what happened.
  3. Context drives behaviour – focus on what conditions the worker faced and what pressure they were under.
  4. Leader response to failure matters – focus on learning. Leaders shape how the organization learns by their reaction to failure. Opening comments are critical.
  5. Learning and improving are vital – workers are not a problem to be solved, but the problem solvers. Promote a safe environment and honest dialogue.
